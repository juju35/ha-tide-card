# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First version: `custom:tide-card` and `custom:tide-mini-card`.
- The curve of the day with the time and height now, the high and low waters as tiles (the next one outlined), the coefficients of the coming 7 days with spring tides in amber.
- Illustrated header: a sea whose level follows the tide, drifting waves, a lighthouse that shines by night (dark theme).
- Adapters for Marées France (tides, measured water levels and coefficients from its websocket commands), NOAA Tides (core and the Flight-Lab / jshufro fork), World Tides (core and custom), UKHO Tides and Norwegian Tide, and a manual `mapping` for anything else.
- Visual editor for both cards, 22 languages.
